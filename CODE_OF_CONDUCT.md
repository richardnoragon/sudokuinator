# Code of conduct

## Expected behavior

Treat participants respectfully. Give specific, constructive feedback about the
work. Welcome newcomers, credit contributions, and respect differing backgrounds,
experience levels, and accessibility needs.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate personal attacks,
and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not
use project discussions to intimidate contributors or repeatedly disrupt their work.

## Scope and reporting

This policy covers repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project
spaces. Report concerns privately to a core maintainer through an available private
contact channel. Role responsibilities are described in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
No dedicated conduct-reporting address is currently published; do not post private
incident details in public issues. A dedicated private contact remains a maintainer
setup task before opening broader community participation.

## Enforcement

Core maintainers review reports, protect reporter privacy as far as practical, and
consider context and severity. Responses may include a request to stop, removal of
content, a warning, or temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
Maintainers involved in an incident should recuse themselves from its review.
